Privacy Policy

Mouse&Keyboard Bridge · Effective 4 November 2026

The short version

Mouse&Keyboard Bridge does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything it does happens between your own computers on your local network.

What the app handles

  • Mouse and keyboard input. While the cursor is on the other computer, the app forwards your mouse movements, clicks, scrolling and key presses to that computer so they take effect there. Input is not recorded or kept.
  • Clipboard text. If "Share clipboard" is on, copied text is sent to your paired computer so you can paste it there. It is not stored anywhere else.
  • Files you choose to send. A file you pick with "Send File…" is transferred to the paired computer's Downloads folder.
  • Settings. The device name, pairing code and screen arrangement are saved locally on each computer.

Network

The app finds the other computer with Bonjour on your local network and connects to it directly. Every connection is end-to-end encrypted with a key derived from your pairing code. The app never sends data to the developer or to any third party, and it does not connect to the internet.

Permissions

On macOS the app asks for the Accessibility permission. It needs this to see your mouse and keyboard and to move the cursor and type on the computer you are controlling. It does not read screen contents or the contents of other apps.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: contact support.
